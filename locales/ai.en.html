A lamp that only wakes at night decides to chase the sunrise.

An AI brand TVC created for ShotLab. Beginning with a single line of text, an anthropomorphic streetlamp travels through Tang-dynasty Chang’an, Republican-era city streets, wartime China and the modern city. Its identity remains consistent across histories and spaces, until it reaches the sea and sees a sunrise for the first time.

Character and scene references, image and video generation, character consistency and a node-based Canvas workflow are woven into the narrative. The product’s capabilities become part of the story itself.

Continuous third-person camera movement and the impression of a single take connect different periods, locations and character forms into one complete journey.
